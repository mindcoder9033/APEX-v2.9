## 2. Allocate Maximum 3 Skills

These skills are derived from the chapter's focus on tire physics and management.

1.  **Skill 1: Slip Angle Management (Cornering)**
    - **Focus:** Training the driver to operate the car at the optimal slip angle for peak cornering grip (5° for slicks, 7° for street tires).
    - **Feedback:** The "Slip Angle Monitor" shows real-time slip angles. If the driver exceeds the optimal zone, a "LOSS OF GRIP" warning appears.
    - **Relevant Text:** *"The fastest driver would operate the car in the range of 5 degrees of slip: not over, not under... Exceeding the optimum slip angle... puts you in a range where the CF is lower." (Page 194)*

2.  **Skill 2: Percent Slip Management (Braking/Acceleration)**
    - **Focus:** Training the driver to maintain ~15% slip under braking and acceleration for peak longitudinal grip.
    - **Feedback:** The "Percent Slip" gauge tracks braking and acceleration slip. If the driver exceeds 15% (lockup or wheelspin), a "TRACTION LOST" warning appears.
    - **Relevant Text:** *"Under braking or acceleration, the tire delivers its optimum traction at a certain range of % of slip... around 15% slip... push too hard and the tires lock up, losing 30-40% of peak traction." (Page 195)*

3.  **Skill 3: Tire Temperature Management**
    - **Focus:** Training the driver to keep tires in the optimal temperature range (200-240°F for slicks) through smooth inputs and balanced cornering.
    - **Feedback:** The "Tire Temperature Monitor" shows real-time temps. The system alerts if the driver is overheating (aggressive slides) or under-using (cold tires) the tires.
    - **Relevant Text:** *"A tire's CF is lower than the peak at cooler temperatures but rises to a maximum at a particular temperature range... It then falls off again as the tire temperature exceeds its design range." (Page 194)*

## 5. Suggest Game Type

- **Suggestion:** **Circuit Race (Open Practice)**
- **Rationale:** The chapter emphasizes experimentation and learning tire characteristics (slip angles, temperatures, percent slip). Open Practice allows unlimited laps to test different lines, brake pressures, and throttle applications without the pressure of competition.

## 6. Time of Day

- **Suggestion:** **Late Morning (10:00 AM)**
- **Rationale:** The chapter discusses how **track temperature** affects tire compound choice (hard vs. soft, Page 194). Late morning provides a consistent, moderate track temperature, reducing variables and allowing the driver to focus on slip angles and percent slip.

## 7. Weather

- **Suggestion:** **Clear**
- **Rationale:** The chapter's lessons on slip angles, percent slip, and temperature management assume a dry track. Wet conditions introduce hydroplaning and significantly alter grip levels, distracting from the core lessons of tire physics.

---

## 8. Number of Laps

- **Suggestion:** **15 Laps**
- **Rationale:** With 15 laps, the driver experiences:
    - 5 laps for Stint 1 (Slip Angle Hunter)
    - 5 laps for Stint 2 (Percent Slip Master)
    - 5 laps for Stint 3 (Temperature Manager)
    - Enough data to see how slip angles, percent slip, and temperatures change over a session.

## 9. Number of Drivatars

- **Suggestion:** **0 Drivatars**
- **Rationale:** The chapter focuses on *tire management*—slip angles, percent slip, and temperature. Other cars add variables (draft, traffic) that complicate the analysis of tire performance. Running alone allows the driver to focus purely on tire behavior.

#### Stint 2: Percent Slip Management

| Event | Optimal % Slip | Your Average % Slip | Result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Braking (Hairpin) | 15% | 22% (lockup) | **Flat spot detected** |
| Acceleration (Turn 9 Exit) | 15% | 18% (wheelspin) | Traction lost |
| Braking (Turn 3) | 15% | 12% | Under-braked |

**Analysis:** You are locking the brakes at the Hairpin (22% slip = lockup), creating a flat spot. You are also spinning the tires on exit (18% slip), losing acceleration traction.

**Coaching:** *"Push too hard on the brake pedal and the percent slip rate goes higher than 15% and traction is lost. The grossest error is pushing so hard that the tires stop rotating altogether. As 'lockup' occurs, the tire loses 30% to 40% of its peak traction." (Page 195)*

**Action Item:** Practice Stint 2. Modulate brake pressure to keep % slip near 15%. On acceleration, squeeze the throttle, don't stomp it.
